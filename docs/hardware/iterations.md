# Design iterations

Naming (reconstructed; TODO: confirm labels): V1 = first print, V2 = fixes of 2026-10-23…28, V3 = redesign from 2026-11-09.

## V1 – printed 2026-10-21, assembled 2026-10-22
**Changed vs. original UMI:** removed GoPro holder, side mirrors, rear box; added adapter on bottom plate for the F/T sensor; new trigger (long, hooks around sensor) and grip; fingers point downwards; ergonomics changed.
**Learned:** it can grasp objects (pen … computer mouse), but: wrong clearance handle/sensor/adapter (hook too short), trigger too long, handle hole ~1 mm too shallow, trigger guide too thick, 1–2 mm play in handle fit → jamming, ~1 cm wobble of finger holders, trigger bent and broke. Top plate print defect. See ISS-001…008.

## V2 – 2026-10-23 → 2026-10-28
**Changed:** deeper handle hole; larger hook distance; shorter trigger (fully inside the handle); thinner trigger in the guide; tighter tolerances for linkages, gears, sled.
**Learned:** everything tested improved (qualitative). Supervisor recommends round hook (ADR-006).
**Open:** TODO: quantify remaining play and trigger force.
