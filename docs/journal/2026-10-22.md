# 2026-10-22 – V1 assembled and tested (reconstructed)

## Done
- Assembled V1.
- Grasp tests with objects from pen size up to computer-mouse size.

## Observed
- Objects can be picked up.
- Clearance between handle, F/T sensor and adapter miscalculated → trigger hook too short, trigger does not move in/out properly (ISS-002).
- Trigger too long: much of it stays outside the handle even at full travel (ISS-003).
- Handle hole for the sensor not deep enough: handle sits ~1 mm too high on the sensor (ISS-004).
- Trigger too thick in the guide in the adapter: jams (ISS-005). Sanding helped only partly (PLA/plastic sands slowly; TODO: confirm material).
- Trigger fit in the handle has ~1–2 mm play: trigger tilts and jams when pressed far up (larger moment) (ISS-006).
- Linkages, gears and sled have too much tolerance: finger holders wobble by almost 1 cm without using the trigger (ISS-007).
- Trigger bent strongly in thin sections and broke (ISS-008).
- Top plate print defect (ISS-001).
