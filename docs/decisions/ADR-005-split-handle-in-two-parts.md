# ADR-005: Split handle into two printed parts

- Date: 2026-11-09 (reconstructed)
- Status: active (V3, not yet printed/tested; TODO: update after print)

## Context
V1/V2 handle printed poorly as one part.

## Options
1. One-piece handle.
2. Two-part handle, better print orientation.

## Decision
Two parts.

## Rationale
Better printability.

## Consequences
- Interface/joint between parts must be designed and checked for stiffness (TODO: verify after print).
